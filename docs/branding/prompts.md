# Luna-chan asset exports

The approved concept was created with the built-in Imagegen tool. Masters were exported with the same tool using `luna-chan-concept.png` as their reference. The PNGs in the integration's `brand/` directory are size-normalized copies of these masters. The initial transparent dark-logo export below was superseded by the final opaque export because automatic background removal left visible artifacts.

## icon

Extract only the standalone circular portrait emblem in the TOP LEFT quadrant of this approved Luna Local branding sheet. Preserve the EXACT original girl's identity, expression, pose, fluffy ears, hair, blue eyes, cream sweater, full moon and starry circular background. Do not redraw, redesign or change age or appearance. Remove everything outside the circular emblem to true alpha transparency. Output a single centered square PNG icon, the full circular emblem touching the bounds without clipping, no text, no padding, no other panels. This is a wholesome fully clothed children's-book anime mascot.

## logo

Extract only the complete LIGHT-BACKGROUND logo lockup in the BOTTOM LEFT quadrant of the approved sheet: circular portrait on the left and dark navy decorative 'Luna Local' wordmark on the right with its crescent and ornaments. Preserve the EXACT original character, expression, sweater, moon, lettering shapes and arrangement, do not redesign or redraw. Remove the off-white background outside the artwork to true alpha transparency, preserving the character's white hair, sweater and moon within the circular emblem. Output a single tightly cropped landscape transparent PNG logo, no other panels or margins. Wholesome fully clothed anime mascot.
